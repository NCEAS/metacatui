Bounding Box Coordinates
Use latitude and longitude to describe the location or area covered by your data. For an area, enter the northwest corner first and the southeast corner second. For example, a rough bounding box for the contiguous United States would use 49, -125 as the northwest corner and 25, -66 as the southeast corner. For a single location, enter just one latitude/longitude pair.
